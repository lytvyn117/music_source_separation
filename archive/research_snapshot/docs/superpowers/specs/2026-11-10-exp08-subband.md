# Exp08 subband separator design

**Status** — Train-only feasibility passed. Normalized complex MSE is frozen as the primary reconstruction objective. The implementation plan awaits review. Exp04 remains the production baseline and Exp07 remains NO-GO.

### Source weighting

Write predictions as V and I = X − V and projected references as Vp and Ip = X − Vp. Then I − Ip = −(V − Vp). Any even penalty on the same linear view of this error, with common normalization and reduction, has identical source losses and gradients. This covers normalized complex L1 and MSE, waveform L1, and stereo side L1. A normalized weighted average gives exactly the one-source objective. An unnormalized weighted sum only scales that term by the sum of the weights, changing its relative strength against other terms. It cannot favor one source.

Errors after separately taking magnitudes or log-magnitudes can differ. Retain their equal average for both sources. The vocal leakage auxiliary instead measures a particular projection of instrumental error onto the projected vocal reference. It has a genuinely vocal-specific gradient. The FP64 regression compares both losses and gradients for equal, unequal, and one-sided weights and verifies the raw-sum scaling. A separate counterexample checks nonlinear magnitude errors.

## Final architecture

44.1 kHz stereo, 441,000 samples per chunk, centered Hann FFT/window 2,048 and hop 512. The STFT has 1,025 bins and 862 frames. Normalize features by one mixture spectral RMS per clip, floored at 1e-4. Pack ReL, ImL, ReR, ImR, log1p(|L|), log1p(|R|). Keep the original complex spectrum for synthesis.

| Stage | Shape or widths | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | [B,2,441000] → complex [B,2,1025,862] → [B,6,1025,862] | Joint stereo feature scale |
| Band split | 64 / 192 / 769 bins | Slices [0:64], [64:256], [256:1025] |
| Encoder 1 | 32 channels × 32 / 48 / 97 bins | Frequency-only kernel=stride 2 / 4 / 8. Pad right by seven in high band. Residual depths 2 / 2 / 1. Save skips |
| Encoder 2 | 128 channels × 16 / 16 / 25 bins | Kernel=stride 2 / 3 / 4. Pad right by three in high band |
| Bottleneck | [B,128,57,T] | Eight temporal blocks with dilation 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128. Frequency mixers before blocks 1 and 5 |
| Decoder | 32 channels × 32 / 48 / 97, then [B,4,1025,T] | Mirrored transposed convolutions. Crop the encoder padding exactly, add encoder-1 skips, apply one local block per band, and add a full-resolution per-band 6→4 pointwise logit path |
| Estimates | complex [B,2,2,1025,T] → [B,2,2,441000] | Source order vocal/instrumental. Channel order L/R |

Each local residual block has per-token channel LayerNorm, 1×1 32→16, SiLU, padded depthwise 3×3, SiLU, and 1×1 16→32. The temporal branch uses 128→256→128 and a symmetric depthwise 1×5. Frequency mixers use per-token channel LayerNorm and a 57→96→57 SiLU MLP shared across channels and frames. All convolutions and linears have biases. Initialize both logit paths with weight standard deviation 0.001 and zero bias.

Interpret logits as (uL,vL,uR,vR). In FP32 set M = (0.5 + 0.65 tanh(u)) + j(0.25 tanh(v)), with imaginary mask zero at DC and Nyquist. Use V = MX and I = X − V. Both masks are bounded by **1.176861×** the mixture magnitude (+1.415 dB). Exactly zero mixture bins produce two zeros. Keep transforms, complex arithmetic, and loss in FP32. Use matched iSTFT lengths and the same positive Hann overlap-add weights at 50% overlap for both stems.

The instantiated reference graph has **653,650 trainable parameters**. Shape checks cover B=2/T=862 and B=1/T=517. Stereo ordering, right padding, DC/Nyquist, silence, and the head bound have automated checks. Enumerating the instantiated temporal kernels' taps verifies **1,021 consecutive offsets** from −510 to +510 (about 11.85 seconds at hop 512). Local encoder and decoder blocks add up to six frames along the longest feature path. Shared chunk RMS also couples all time frames through the input scale.

A deterministic BF16 CUDA smoke with FP32 transforms/loss, synthetic stereo, microbatch 2, four warm-up and ten timed updates measured **0.237 s median/step**, **8.39 clips/s**, and **3.20 GiB allocated / 3.44 GiB reserved** on the 3080 Ti. Every parameter tensor received nonzero finite gradients. The maximum complex error was 0.067× its allowance, waveform peak residual 9.69e-8, and head bound excess zero. All 14 synthetic steps clipped at norm 1, with raw norms 9.08–12.04. The timing excludes data loading, validation, and post-update diagnostics. Both throughput and clipping must be checked on the integrated real-data workload.

## Frozen training objective

Use original recorded mixture X. For the loss, project the original references to add to X with equal correction, then construct Ip by complement. The fixed validation objective is the same as the training objective. Original stems remain the scoring references.

- **1.0 × normalized complex MSE** on V − Vp. For each example divide by max(stereo spectral RMS of X, 1e-4), then average squared complex magnitude over both channels, bins, and frames and average examples. This sums real and imaginary squared error per complex coefficient. An average per real component would be half as large.
- **0.5 × balanced log-magnitude L1** using the same spectral scale. Average the two sources after taking log1p of scaled magnitudes.
- **2.0 × waveform L1** and **0.25 × side L1**, each once on the common projected waveform error. Side is (L−R)/2. Use the same per-example mixture waveform RMS floor of 1e-4 for both.
- **0.25 × vocal bleed auxiliary**. On nonoverlapping one-second frames where projected vocal RMS exceeds 0.005, regress instrumental error onto the projected vocal across both stereo channels. Average squared positive excess of the absolute coefficient over 0.05. Use zero when no frame qualifies. The absolute value discourages both retained vocal and over-subtraction.

Do not introduce per-source multipliers. Keep these coefficients frozen for the planned run. A failed real-data gradient gate returns to design review.

## Training, evaluation, and promotion

- Use the existing seed-42 **85/15** split of the official training folder. Freeze ordered track IDs, WAV fingerprints, source fingerprints, baseline checkpoint hash, and val clip and listening manifests. Exclude official test audio from development. Pretrained SCNet remains excluded pending checkpoint-specific proof of zero recording overlap.
- Start from scratch at seed 42. Use 2,048 ten-second clips per epoch, microbatch 2 with four-step accumulation, AdamW betas (0.9, 0.999), peak LR 3e-4, weight decay 1e-4, and global gradient clip 1.0 once per optimizer update. Warm up over 256 optimizer steps and cosine-decay to 1e-5 over 60 epochs. Reuse existing remix, active sampling and gain ranges, use joint L/R swaps, and derive augmentation draws from seed/epoch/index. Use deterministic CUDA with CUBLAS_WORKSPACE_CONFIG=:4096:8 for resume and measured training.
- Use 150 fixed unaugmented ten-second validation clips, ten per song including quiet and vocal-active sections. Save epoch zero. Select minimum full val_total_loss including leakage with min_delta 1e-4 and patience 12. Persist model, optimizer, scheduler, sampler, RNG, selection, history, and fingerprints at epoch boundaries. Publish the latest and selected checkpoint through a recoverable atomic commit. Save snapshots every ten epochs.
- After implementation, profile 100 optimizer updates on real training clips at peak LR after eight untimed profiling updates. Require ≥8 clips/s including input pipeline and production guards, process peak reserved ≤8 GiB, and ≥2 GiB device headroom. Require finite losses, gradients and outputs, nonzero gradients in all branches, zero mask/additivity violations, and at most 50 of the 100 timed updates clipped. If batch 2 exceeds memory limits, reprofile batch 1 with accumulation 8. Any failed gate blocks the full run. The reference graph's synthetic clipping rate is not evidence that the real-data gradient gate will pass.
- Stop before updating on nonfinite results, a head bound violation, or a complex additivity error above 1e-5 + 4×2^-24×(|X|+|V|+|I|). Check waveform additivity after iSTFT and overlap-add with peak residual ≤2e-5×max(1,peak|x|). Log clipped samples, gain, DC, side energy, and head saturation. Preserve the last valid checkpoint and record failed-batch metadata separately.
- Evaluate the single selected checkpoint on the 15 full songs against unchanged Exp04. Use the original mixture and original stems, matching metrics and 50% overlap. Primary native chunk lengths are Exp04 six seconds and Exp08 ten seconds. Report matched six-second inference as a sensitivity check. Score per song, report paired deltas and song-bootstrap intervals, and export 12 prespecified blind A/B listening sets as float stereo WAV at common unity gain.

|---|---|
| Median vocal SDR | ≥7.2415 dB |
| Median instrumental SDR | ≥13.0874 dB |
