1. **Verify recording identity.** After licensed access, capture archive, original audio, canonical decoded PCM and metadata SHA-256 hashes, source links, artist aliases, provider, release/session and known remixes. Compare title/artist/provider and canonical PCM hashes against the frozen 85/15, exposed demos where available, and candidate tracks against each other. Run offset-aware landmark audio matching on several separated portions of mixes and vocal groups in both directions. Compare backing when a remix or sample is suspected. Validate sensitivity on known local positives with gain changes, AAC recoding, resampling and trimmed excerpts, and on known negative pairs. Manually confirm borderline matches from consistent offsets and aligned audio. Exclude reused performances, alternate mixes of the same takes, shared vocal recordings, and unresolved matches. Record findings for every candidate. Use the official roster for name exclusion only.
2. **Build references.** Check for missing files, mic bleed, mislabeled stems and wet vocal returns routed into backing. Sum all singing voices, lead and backing, using the pinned stem map. Sum remaining musical accompaniment. Resolve mixed voice/instrument stems before freezing or exclude the recording. Use original stereo processed stems on their common sample grid. For the primary tier construct a stem-sum mix from the two references with unit weights on the supplied stems and apply one common gain to mixture and both reference groups only if needed to keep all peaks at or below 0.98. Record the gain. Use the supplied original mix as a separately reported sensitivity tier only where the same aligned references account for it within 1% stereo residual NRMSE after documented provider gains, with the same common gain treatment. The public coefficient table has no entries for the 17 candidates, so eligibility for the original-mix tier is unresolved. This primary tier estimates generalization to unseen stem-sum mixtures.
3. **Freeze the manifest.** Before inference, seal ordered IDs, licenses, exclusions, hashes, source maps, sample counts, gains, code/environment, output paths, metrics, bootstrap seed, listening offsets and decision thresholds. Include all surviving tracks. Proceed with a decision-level study only with at least 14 songs from 12 artists and at least 12 eligible listening excerpts. Below that floor, mark the study inconclusive and require a new frozen manifest before scoring a replacement pool. Record sample counts and metric eligibility per song. Do not replace tracks based on model output.
4. **Run one paired comparison.** Use the verified [freeze manifest](../../../reports/exp04_exp08_hybrid_review_20261111/freeze_manifest.json) for Exp04 epoch 19 and Exp08 epoch 53. The audit rehashed both checkpoints, all 15 source and review files listed and 84 blind WAVs against that manifest. Use the native [hybrid wrapper](../../../src/experiments/exp04_exp08_hybrid/inference.py) and its Exp04 entry point in deterministic FP32 with seed 42. Native chunks are 6 s/batch 8 and 10 s/batch 2 respectively, each at 50% overlap. Exp04 delivers both native outputs. The hybrid vocal is the waveform mean of Exp04 and Exp08 vocals and its instrumental is the original input minus the mean. Feed both methods the same 44.1 kHz stereo float inputs. Verify exact lengths, channel order, scale, finite outputs and sample alignment. Require hybrid peak reconstruction residual ≤2e-5 × max(1, peak of input). Log Exp04's known native boundary residual and interior residual. Keep audio at native output scale.
5. **Report paired results.** Primary metric is vocal SDR, using the existing complete one-second reference frames with RMS ≥−45 dBFS and the median per track. Report vocal SI-SDR, instrumental SDR, signed and absolute vocal bleed and its squared coefficient, reverse contamination, joint reference fit, below-threshold vocal output, quiet and buried strata, side SDR, side RMS error, gain, peaks, clipping, mixture residual and throughput. Use 10,000 paired artist-cluster bootstrap draws with seed 42 and report the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Resample artists with replacement and retain their tracks in each draw. Report marginal medians, median paired deltas, win/tie/loss counts, all per-song results and exclusions. Report the legacy song bootstrap as a sensitivity statistic.
6. **Listen blind.** Use 12 reference-selected 20-second excerpts from distinct artists, four emphasizing quiet/buried vocals, four dense coactivity, and four sparse or vocal-inactive transitions. Enumerate complete 20-second reference windows starting on five-second boundaries. Rank quiet/buried windows by the count of eligible quiet or buried one-second frames from section 4, and dense windows by the count of active near-parity frames. Require at least five qualifying frames in those two strata. For transitions, require at least five frames on either side of the −45 dBFS vocal activity threshold, then rank by the number of threshold crossings. Process quiet, dense and transition strata in that order, reserving distinct artists. Break track ties by ascending SHA-256 of seed 42 and track ID, then by earliest offset. If a stratum is short, record the shortfall before the freeze and fill from the next eligible distinct artists in the remaining ranked windows. A panel below 12 excerpts leaves the study incomplete. Randomize A/B with six A placements per model for each reviewer, use exact slices of the full-song renders, provide reference and mixture, and keep one playback level with enhancements and normalization disabled. Three listeners score clarity, backing quality, bleed, stereo and artifacts before key reveal. An excerpt-level win uses majority preference and a tie counts as equal for instrumental noninferiority. Record artifact timestamps.
7. **Apply the decision rule.** Retain the original investigation point gates. Both median vocal SDR and paired median vocal SDR delta must improve, median signed and absolute bleed must decline, and both instrumental SDR comparisons must be at least −0.10 dB. For a promotion recommendation, also require lower 95% bounds above zero for the paired vocal improvement and above −0.10 dB for instrumental SDR, and an upper 95% bound below zero for paired absolute bleed change. Require ≥8/12 clearer vocal preferences, ≥8/12 lower-bleed preferences, ≥10/12 equal-or-better instrumentals, and no reproducible material new artifact confirmed by at least two reviewers at the same timestamp. Median absolute side level error may rise by at most 0.25 dB per stem on eligible references (side RMS ≥−50 dBFS). Median clipped fraction may rise by at most 0.01 percentage points per stem, and a previously unclipped track/stem must stay at or below 0.01%. Any failure or inconclusive interval retains Exp04. Record the promotion decision separately.

## 4. Bottleneck analysis

### Spectral results on the 15 development songs

Values are vocal spectral SDR in dB and error cosine for the component models. Band inclusion requires ≥0.01% of a song's vocal reference spectral energy. There are 12 eligible songs below 80 Hz and 15 elsewhere.

| Band | Exp04 | Exp08 | Hybrid | Exp04/08 vocal error cosine |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0–80 Hz | 0.00 | −0.25 | −0.01 | 0.909 |
| 80–250 Hz | 2.69 | 2.61 | 2.99 | 0.844 |
| 250 Hz–1 kHz | 6.72 | 7.49 | 7.56 | 0.806 |
| 1–4 kHz | 6.39 | 5.65 | 6.48 | 0.778 |
| 4–8 kHz | 3.90 | 3.98 | 4.63 | 0.755 |
| 8 kHz–Nyquist | 5.70 | 6.47 | 6.82 | 0.699 |

1. **Recover vocal structure under accompaniment, especially 80 Hz–1 kHz.** This is the largest measurable bottleneck. A read-only calculation on the 12 saved, preselected development excerpts attributed 77.0% of Exp04 and 74.5% of hybrid vocal spectral squared error to 80 Hz–1 kHz. The 250 Hz–1 kHz band alone accounted for 60.7% and 58.7%. The sub-80 Hz band held about 0.2% of reference energy and under 1% of error in this panel. In a two-reference waveform fit, the residual orthogonal to both reference waveforms represented a median 69.2% of Exp04 and 67.4% of hybrid vocal squared error. The hybrid median aligned attenuation term was 19.9% and the backing term 7.5%. These are separate medians and the source terms can covary. The residual can include time-varying leakage, phase and timbre errors. It provides no unique attribution to one cause.
2. **Quiet and overlapping vocals expose the failure.** Exp04's mask-to-ideal-ratio MAE was 0.231 in overlapping time-frequency bins versus 0.176 in vocal-dominant and 0.072 in backing-dominant bins. On the saved excerpts, quiet-active frames (−45 to −30 dBFS, 25 frames from eight songs) had vocal SDR 1.92 / 2.46 / 2.44 dB for Exp04 / Exp08 / hybrid. Buried vocal frames (active and vocal-to-instrumental ratio <−12 dB, 29 frames from seven songs) scored 1.97 / 2.31 / 2.56 dB, and the hybrid aligned vocal coefficient was 0.50. Near parity (±6 dB vocal-to-instrumental ratio, 124 frames from 11 songs), SDR was 6.51 / 6.58 / 6.87 dB. On 31 below-threshold frames in eight excerpts, vocal output RMS was −68.7 / −41.9 / −45.2 dBFS. That last stratum can include real low-level vocals and reports output energy. Frame metrics use a median within each eligible excerpt followed by a median across excerpts. They describe these excerpts and are observational.
3. **Cross-source leakage remains in both directions.** Whole-song vocal bleed in the instrumental was 21.39% / 17.63% / 18.72%, equivalent to 4.58% / 3.11% / 3.51% aligned projection energy. The joint-fit instrumental coefficient in estimated vocals was 0.064 / 0.075 / 0.069. At 250 Hz–1 kHz that backing coefficient was 0.171 / 0.201 / 0.183. Exp08's lower full-band vocal bleed coexists with higher backing contamination in vocals and higher vocal bleed in the 1–4 and 4–8 kHz bands.
4. **Gain and stereo reveal secondary defects.** Whole-song vocal RMS gain was −0.70 / −0.59 / −0.88 dB. Whole-song aligned vocal coefficients were 0.786 / 0.824 / 0.811, while the fraction of active frames with coefficient <0.8 was 52.3% / 52.1% / 53.8%. The hybrid retained less of the aligned vocal waveform in the 4–8 kHz presence band, with coefficient 0.719 versus 0.762 for Exp04. RMS includes backing and residual error. Vocal side SDR was 3.24 / 3.06 / 3.58 dB and median absolute vocal side level error was 2.09 / 1.33 / 2.05 dB. Instrumental side SDR was 10.06 / 10.00 / 10.45 dB. A more accurate side level can coexist with a less accurate side waveform.
5. **The head has a ceiling, and the fitted model remains far below its demonstrated headroom.** Exp04's real softmax masks reuse mixture phase. Exp08 can rotate phase, but constrains Re(M) to [−0.15, 1.15], Im(M) to [−0.25, 0.25] and both source-mask magnitudes to ≤1.177×. Exactly zero mixture bins yield zero outputs, and the head cannot recover large mutually cancelling sources. On the Exp08 train-only 16-clip probe, 37.1% of ideal mask bins were outside the box, carrying 11.1% of projected two-source power. The bounded spectral fit still scored 17.51 dB vocal SDR versus 8.24 dB Exp04 on that panel, with a paired median gain of +9.63 dB. The unbounded projected target reached 34.63 dB. These are oracle references under that procedure. Separately, the development oracle gave 9.30 dB with mixture phase and 31.09 dB with a mixture-consistent complex projection against original stems. Recorded mix/stem mismatch was 2.64% median NRMSE. The observed headroom points to learning source structure before adjusting bounds.
6. **Shared component errors limit the hybrid.** Median full-waveform error cosine was 0.812 vocal and 0.813 instrumental. The vocal cosine was highest at low frequencies and declined above 8 kHz. The blend cancels some differing errors, which explains part of the observed lift. It retains the dominant correlated errors.

Excerpt calculation used the archived float WAVs, no gain changes, a Hann 2048-point FFT with hop 512 and one-sided power weighting, pooling squared error by band across the 12 excerpts. The full-song spectral table is the archived 4096-point, hop-2048 evaluation. Treat the excerpt energy shares as panel-specific.

## 5. One proposed Exp09 direction

**Verify and adapt Sony Research's 12.1M-parameter, four-second single-pass BS-RoFormer variant 1, release v1.0.0 `bsroformer_small.zip`.** Hypothesis: learned time and cross-band context will recover vocal structure in 80 Hz–1 kHz and improve buried-vocal SDR while preserving backing and stereo. Pretrained weights let the project test that hypothesis before spending GPU hours. Sony reports 9.64 dB vocals and 16.80 dB accompaniment with museval on MUSDB18-HQ test. Those results motivate the candidate. Different recordings and metrics preclude a direct score comparison with our 15-song results. The public report says MUSDB18-HQ official train and supplies a release artifact. Checkpoint-specific validation, selection and weight-use terms still need verification.

| Direction considered | Evidence, cost and risk |
| --- | --- |
| Data-only expansion | HQ restores the uncompressed signal and extends bandwidth from roughly 16 to 22 kHz, with zero new recording identities. Diverse, clean multitracks could help generalization, but only newly licensed and deduplicated recordings change diversity. A fixed-architecture experiment fits the card at Exp08's measured 3.46 GiB reserved and about four hours at its recorded budget, plus curation and storage. Separate HQ fidelity from diversity in any later ablation |
| Stronger architecture from scratch | BSRNN, BS-RoFormer and SCNet show that cross-band and temporal modeling can work. HT Demucs shows sensitivity to data scale. Reproducing a published BS-RoFormer training budget is impractical here: the selected variant used 1M updates at batch 96. A compact local pilot is feasible after profiling, but its cost and quality are uncertain, with 85 training recordings and no inherited weights |
| Verified pretrained adaptation | The released small model matches the two-stem task and provides an inexpensive zero-shot control. A small tail update fits a bounded budget if profiling confirms it. Primary risks are checkpoint training/selection exposure, usage terms, genre and codec shift, and overfitting during adaptation |
| Another mask or blend adjustment | Low implementation cost. The bounded oracle already demonstrates large headroom and the measured component errors are highly correlated. Existing results support studying the vocal representation before another blend or mask change |

Sources [Sony checkpoint and published scores](https://github.com/SonyResearch/mimo-audio-separation#-pretrained-checkpoints), [release](https://github.com/SonyResearch/mimo-audio-separation/releases/tag/v1.0.0), [Sony paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07226v1), [BSRNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15174), [SCNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13276), [Hybrid Transformer Demucs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08553), [MUSDB18-HQ](https://zenodo.org/records/3338373).

### Proposed implementation sequence

- [ ] **Bind provenance and reserves.** Pin the Sony release, code commit, dependency versions, checkpoint and config hashes and weight-use terms. Record the exact training and validation rosters, checkpoint selection rule and any auxiliary, pseudo-labeled, or evaluation-driven selection exposures. The publisher's reported HQ training scope implies that our 15 development recordings must be presumed exposed to this checkpoint. Use those 15 for diagnostics only. Any unresolved intersection with the independent sets or official-test-driven checkpoint selection blocks this experiment. If publisher evidence is insufficient, record a provenance NO-GO.
- [ ] **Prepare data for the one experiment.** Use the current 85-song MUSDB18 training partition for adaptation, with the existing codec version to match the local target domain and to isolate the effect of the weight update against the frozen zero-shot arm. Use an artist-disjoint 20-song external development panel from provider-licensed MoisesDB v0.1, after license/recording checks and with Sony's published family-level MoisesDB evaluation disclosed. Restrict MoisesDB to development and review its noncommercial research terms. Before tuning, reserve a separate final panel of at least 24 full songs from at least 20 artists with direct written permission covering research, stems and any intended sharing. Source this panel from newly contributed private artist mixes with documented take or session dates after the pinned checkpoint release. Require aligned 44.1 kHz stereo vocal and backing stems, verify all recording and artist exclusions against both pipelines' fit/selection data and the development panel, and keep it sealed. The intake and permissions are open dependencies. The MedleyDB pool used for the Exp04/hybrid decision has its own completed purpose once unblinded. [Music AI research terms](https://music.ai/research/#datasets) · [MoisesDB stem taxonomy and integrity hashes](https://github.com/moises-ai/moises-db).
- [ ] **Implement isolated inference and prove parity.** Proposed files are `src/experiments/exp09_pretrained/{inference,data,train,evaluate}.py` with focused tests under `tests/exp09`. Reuse the local scorer, paired exports, and checkpoint-selection and atomic-resume logic. For the candidate, preserve the verified checkpoint's native input normalization and invert it after inference. Freeze 4 s chunks, 50% overlap, batch 1 and FP32 for the formal comparison, with the candidate vocal and a complementary instrumental. Match the upstream vocal path on a fixed fixture before comparisons. Test exact sample alignment, silence, stereo, additivity, peaks, one short track and one irregular tail. Reject datasets with nonmanifest IDs and runs that overwrite existing output paths. Keep all existing Exp01–08 artifacts protected by before/after hashes.
- [ ] **Run zero-shot first on the external development panel.** Compare the candidate to Exp04 and the unchanged hybrid with the same inputs, metrics and paired deltas. Continue to adaptation only if paired median vocal SDR improves by ≥0.50 dB over the hybrid, instrumental SDR loses ≤0.10 dB, median absolute vocal bleed rises by ≤0.5 pp, and signal and stereo guardrails pass. Log performance on the 80 Hz–1 kHz band and quiet/buried frames. Validate usable headroom with a timed 100-update real-audio profile before any longer approved run.
- [ ] **Test one adaptation.** Freeze all but the last time/frequency transformer block and vocal mask decoder. Fix seed 42, BF16 forward/backward with FP32 transforms and loss, microbatch 1, accumulation 8, four-second crops and AdamW at 1e-5 with weight decay 1e-4 and global norm clipping at 1.0. Use 256 optimizer updates per epoch and a maximum of 10 epochs (2,560 updates), with patience 3. Use stereo waveform L1 + normalized complex L1 against the equal-residual projected vocal target. Compute complex L1 as mean modulus of STFT error divided by max(1e-4, mixture STFT RMS), with Hann 2048/hop 512. Waveform L1 uses the same convention with waveform RMS and unit term weights. Select minimum mean combined loss on 120 fixed external development crops (six per song), including quiet and overlapping sections, with min_delta 1e-4. Preserve the epoch-0 candidate and save only epoch-boundary best/latest plus epochs 5 and 10 if reached. Record data, RNG, code, optimizer, selection and state hashes and require exact epoch-boundary replay before a sustained run. Zero-shot and adaptation use the same inference adapter. Adopt updated weights only for ≥0.25 dB paired median vocal gain over zero-shot with a positive lower 95% bound on external development, all previous quality/signal gates, and no worse than −0.10 dB instrumental paired median versus zero-shot. Otherwise the frozen weights are the experiment's result. Freeze the single resulting candidate before any final score.
- [ ] **Run one final evaluation.** Compare the fixed candidate to both baselines on the sealed panel. Report all per-track and listening outcomes, artist-cluster uncertainty and subgroup sizes. Archive a decision and protected-file verification. A separately recorded production decision follows successful evaluation.

**Exp09 quality GO** requires all of the following on the sealed final panel.

- Paired median vocal SDR gain ≥1.0 dB versus both Exp04 and the hybrid, with 95% lower bound >0 against each. Candidate wins on at least 70% of songs versus the hybrid.
- Paired median instrumental SDR gain ≥0.50 dB versus the hybrid, with a 95% lower bound >−0.10 dB. At most 10% of songs lose >1 dB on either stem versus the hybrid.
- Median absolute vocal bleed improves ≥2.0 pp versus the hybrid, and median signed bleed does not rise. Median 80 Hz–1 kHz vocal spectral SDR improves ≥1.0 dB and the buried-vocal stratum improves ≥0.75 dB versus the hybrid. Require buried-vocal coverage from at least eight artists and report quiet-active and below-threshold diagnostics. No worsening >1 dB of median below-threshold vocal output RMS where the reference is below −45 dBFS.
- The frozen listening rule in section 3 passes against the hybrid. Side, clipping, additivity and finite/alignment gates pass. Inference speed and peak memory are reported with the same timing boundary as the baseline comparison.

A miss or insufficient qualifying coverage is **NO-GO for promotion**. Verification and the zero-shot gate determine whether a training proposal is warranted. Final scores inform the decision on the frozen candidate.
